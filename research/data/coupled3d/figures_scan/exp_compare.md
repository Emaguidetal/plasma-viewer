The He anchors match pressure and nominal field values only. The project cavity is at 2.95 GHz; the published compressor is about 2.767 GHz, with different geometry, seed and detector. Published field values are calculated from power; peak versus RMS is unclear. The measured +30 ± 15 ns is a laser-to-light-onset difference between output-power groups; ±15 ns is the sum of the reported jitters. Field-collapse and light-onset delays are different observables.
